Good to know

Support centered on long-term success.

Who are services designed for?

Individuals with disabilities who want personalized help building workplace skills, preparing for employment, or maintaining meaningful work.

What does one-on-one support mean?

Guidance is shaped around each person’s strengths, goals, abilities, and learning needs instead of using one standard plan.

Does support continue after employment begins?

Yes. Continued support is part of our approach to help individuals understand expectations, strengthen workplace success, and maintain employment.
